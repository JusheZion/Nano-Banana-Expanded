# Hub & overview

_Last reviewed: 2026-04-05_

The **Overview** portal is the ARCS landing page: hero, version badge, and quick-access cards into each creative area.

## Navigation

- **Sidebar** (left): expand on hover to see labels. Entries under **Creative Suite** mirror the cards on the landing page.
- **Account** (sidebar footer): **Sign in** opens the Supabase-backed session when URL + anon key are configured. Hover the avatar for **Sign out**.
- **Portals Wiki** (this site section): opened from the sidebar or the landing grid — documentation only; **no sign-in required** to read articles.
