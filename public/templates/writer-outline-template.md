# Writer Outline Paste Template

## Guidance

- Title and Premise are optional.
- Acts are optional.
- Paste a structured outline or ordinary prose; ordinary prose is accepted and reviewable.
- Separators may use an ASCII hyphen (-), en dash (–), or em dash (—).
- Page beats may target an individual page; Pages X-Y ranges expand to one proposed beat per page.

## Optional Title and Premise

**TITLE:** Optional story title

**PREMISE:** Optional one-sentence premise

## Optional Acts

**ACTS:**

- Act I - Opening: Establish the ordinary world and the first disruption.
- Act II – Confrontation: Escalate the conflict and force a difficult choice.
- Act III — Return: Resolve the central conflict and show what changed.

## Page Beats

- Page 1 — Opening image: Introduce the setting and protagonist.
- Page 2 - Inciting turn: Disrupt the protagonist’s routine.
- Pages 4-5 — Sequence: Let a connected scene span a page range.

## Notes

**NOTES:** Preserve the quiet tone and leave room for a wordless reaction.

## Intentionally Unassigned Prose

This intentionally unassigned prose has no structural label so it can be reviewed and placed later.
