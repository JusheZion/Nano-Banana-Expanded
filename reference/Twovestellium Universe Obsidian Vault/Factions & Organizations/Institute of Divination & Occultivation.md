# Overview
One of the most prestigious educational institutions in the world that encompasses all major fields of learning including the latest developments in artificial intelligence, the classical sciences, the disciplines of individuation and the principles of unity
  
# Purpose
As one of the first institutions to formally accept the occult arts and principles of divination as educational fields of study, the research into the connections between the classical sciences, the occult arts, and the frontier discoveries in AI has been instrumental in continuing to further our knowledge and sustain the utopic society the unification of knowledge has ushered in
