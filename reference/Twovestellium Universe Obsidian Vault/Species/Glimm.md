#About - The Glimm are reanimated humans created through scientific procedures with the intent of granting the subjects of the abilities of the TwoveLux without the spiritual, mental and physical training that occurs to use the abilities.  The perverse experience  drains all life energy from the human host and funnels it into a synthetic heart-the source of their existence and power.  Once the life is consumed, the body is restructured from dead tissue. Muscles are stretched into skin. Organs are displaced. Bones are warped. The new heart animates the remains.  Glimms are mindless, move awkwardly, lacking coordination and hampered by the random placement of bones and muscles. Their artificial power source is not unlimited, and thus they exist only to replenish their hearts by absorbing the energy of a Stellarian.  Their presence usually indicates the presence of  Stellarian in the vicinity.  Glimm usually won't expend energy to attack normal humans, and will ignore humans when there energy is low.  Once a Glimm runs out of power, they are absorbed by the Shadows.
