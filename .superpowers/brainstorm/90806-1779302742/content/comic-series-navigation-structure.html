Guided Comics: Series And Issue Entry Layer

Three possible structures for the new cover-led entry flow before Issue Lightbox, Page Production, and Panel Focus.

+
Entry
Series Cover Gallery

A. Cover Gallery First

Opening the comic portal always shows a gallery of series covers, including a blank “new series” cover. Selecting one pushes it into a focused series stage.

B. Last Series First

The portal reopens directly to the last active series focus stage, with a smaller “All series” gallery affordance nearby.

+
Split entry
Series + Recent Issue

C. Hybrid Shelf

A persistent shelf combines series covers and recent issues in one entry screen, then opens the selected context.

My recommendation

Start with A. Cover Gallery First. It best matches your “actual gallery of comic covers” idea, keeps the portal emotionally visual before exposing controls, and gives the blank new-series cover a natural permanent place without turning the entry screen into a dashboard.
