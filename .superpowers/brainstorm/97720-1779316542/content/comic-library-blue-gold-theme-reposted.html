Design rule

The portal can own a blue-to-gold lighting system, but individual covers should not become blue/gold cards. Covers keep real comic-cover proportions, sharp edges, art/title composition, shadows, and spine-like depth. Blue and gold appear in the desk, lighting, active states, dividers, and primary actions.
